Our Story

A Benchmark of Quality & Trust

Based in Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu, Diamond Group is founded on an unwavering commitment to authenticity, longevity, and meticulous attention to detail.

Through our flagship enterprise, Asha Furniture, we handcraft solid wood furniture, traditional cots, and bespoke architectural woodwork that transforms residences, luxury resorts, and corporate offices into spaces of timeless distinction.

Operating strategically from Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu, we leverage seasoned artisan workshops and dedicated craftsmanship to execute projects across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, pan-India, and for overseas clients.
