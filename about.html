Our Story

Born from Kerala’s Greatest Traditions

Kerala has inspired the world for millennia through two extraordinary gifts: its peerless high-altitude aromatic spices and the timeless architectural elegance of its wood masters.

At Diamond Group, we set out with a clear purpose: to eliminate middleman dilution and bring verified, grade-certified Kerala spices directly to domestic and international wholesale markets, while simultaneously creating heirloom solid hardwood furniture designed for durability and beauty.

Headquartered strategically at Kundanoor, Cochin (Kerala - 682304), we leverage direct access to Kochi’s international shipping lanes, national logistics corridors, and local timber seasoning facilities.
