Custom Furniture & Architectural Blueprints

Asha Furniture is not just a showroom of ready-made products. We are a traditional Kerala woodcraft workshop equipped to translate your architectural blueprints, interior designer specifications, or hand-drawn sketches into heirloom solid wood furniture.

Choose How Your Custom Order is Delivered

Your Wood. Your Finish.

Natural / Unfinished Delivery: Highly preferred by architects and homeowners who want complete transparency into timber authenticity and freedom to coordinate on-site finishing with other interior woodwork.

Eligible Pieces Cots & Headboards, Dining Tables & Bench Seating, Executive Desks, TV & Media Consoles, Temple Units (Pooja Mandir), Traditional Kerala Diwans.
Input Formats AutoCAD / PDF blueprints, SketchUp 3D models, Pinterest inspiration photos, or hand-drawn dimensional sketches.
Joinery Techniques Authentic traditional mortise & tenon joinery, heavy-duty solid framing, reinforced interlocking corners.
Timber Sourcing Timber sourced from Kerala. Verified solid wood construction without hollow fillers.
Workshop Location Kundanoor, Cochin, Kerala (open for direct workshop visits and mid-build inspections).
Mid-Build Workshop Inspection: For custom orders, you or your interior designer are invited to our Kundanoor workshop to inspect the structural framing and carving in its raw wooden state before any finishing coat is applied.

Simple 4-Step Process

From Your Vision to Handcrafted Reality

Whether you need a bespoke king-size cot with heirloom floral carvings or a 10-seater dining suite fitted to your exact dining room floor plan.

01

Share Your Blueprint or Idea

Send your room plan, CAD drawing, Pinterest concept, or hand sketch via WhatsApp or email. Specify preferred dimensions and features.

02

Technical & Wood Consultation

Our master craftsmen review joinery, timber suitability, structural weight loads, and provide an accurate cost and timeline estimate.

03

Handcrafting & Mid-Build Inspection

Wood is shaped, carved, and assembled in Kundanoor. You are welcome to visit mid-production to inspect the raw wood and hand-carved motifs.

04

Finished Your Way & Delivered

Delivered in raw natural state for your own finishing team, or custom polished in-house to your desired stain, sealed, and securely transported.

The Asha Furniture Differentiator

Your Furniture. Your Finish. Your Choice.

One of our strongest differentiators: we don't force you into a one-size-fits-all factory coating.

Choice 01 • Natural / Unfinished

Natural / Unfinished Furniture

Receive your custom furniture in its natural, unfinished, finely machine-sanded state.

  • Inspect 100% genuine solid wood without stains concealing knots or joints.
  • Perfect for customers who wish to match existing room woodwork.
  • Enables your preferred polisher to apply bespoke French polish or PU sealants.
Choice 02 • Turnkey Polished

Custom Polished & Sealed

Our experienced in-house finishers polish and coat the furniture according to your vision.

  • Choose from rich dark mahogany, warm walnut, honey teak, or espresso shades.
  • Select your preferred sheen: Natural Matte, Silky Satin, or Gloss.
  • Multi-layer protective coatings that resist moisture and daily wear.
