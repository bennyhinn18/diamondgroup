Handcrafted Traditional Wooden Cot

Bring timeless craftsmanship into your home with a handcrafted wooden cot featuring intricate traditional carving and a distinctive architectural design. Made to order, with flexibility in dimensions and finishing preferences.

Choose Your Finishing Preference

Your Wood. Your Finish.

Natural Unfinished: Allows you to inspect the 100% solid wood and joinery directly, or independently engage your trusted polisher.

Headboard Carving Elaborate carved floral centerpiece, decorative scrollwork, symmetrical lattice pattern, raised ornamental crest, and rounded finials.
Footboard Design Structured rectangular panel arrangement, square/open rectangular center sections, solid framing, and rounded corner posts.
Sleeping Platform Confirmed 100% Solid-Wood sleeping platform (no veneer, no compromise).
Frame & Rails Thick wooden side rails, substantial solid corner posts, precision traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery.
Timber Sourcing Sourced from Kerala. Handcrafted at our workshop in Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu.
Available Sizes King Size (72" x 78"), Queen Size (60" x 78"), or Custom Tailored Dimensions.
Visit & Inspect in Person: We welcome customers, architects, and interior designers to visit our workshop (by appointment) to touch the raw wood, verify the hand-carved motifs, and inspect the sturdy platform before finishing.
