The Diamond Furniture Standard

Why Our Solid Hardwood Furniture Excels

Our multi-stage timber curing and joinery protocol ensures that every table, sofa, and bed remains structurally pristine in tropical coastal climates.

1. Kiln-Dried Moisture Control

Timber is seasoned to optimum 8%-12% moisture in computer-controlled kilns, eliminating any future warping, bending, or weather cracking.

2. Deep Anti-Termite Treatment

Pressure vacuum impregnation with non-hazardous organic wood preservatives protects the heartwood against borers and termites for a lifetime.

3. Mortise & Tenon Joinery

We avoid weak nails or cheap staples. Master joiners employ precision dowels, interlocking mortise-tenon, and dovetailed wooden corner blocks.

4. Multi-Layer Polyurethane Finish

Sanded through 8 abrasive grades and finished with heat-and-scratch resistant coatings that reveal the luminous 3D chatoyancy of mahogany.
