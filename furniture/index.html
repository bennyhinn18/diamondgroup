Handcrafted Traditional Wooden Cot

Every detail is carved by skilled woodworkers rather than stamped out by automated machinery. The headboard and footboard feature a balanced architectural composition designed to command the bedroom.

Distinctive Architectural Features:

  • Headboard: Elaborate carved floral centrepiece, decorative scrollwork, symmetrical lattice-pattern carving, raised ornamental crest, and rounded finials.
  • Footboard: Structured rectangular panel arrangement with square/open rectangular center sections and solid wooden corner posts.
  • Sleeping Platform: Confirmed 100% solid-wood sleeping platform (no particle board or hollow fillers).
  • Structural Rigidity: Substantial corner posts and thick solid-wood side rails.

Natural / Unfinished Furniture

Delivered in its finely machine-sanded, unpolished natural wooden state.

  • Complete Timber Transparency: Inspect every grain and carving without stains.
  • Your Finishing Choice: Independently polish with a professional of your choice.
  • Interior Harmony: Match the wood stain to existing room cabinetry and doors.
  • Architects & Designers: Retain full creative control over custom sheen and oils.

Custom Polished Furniture

Our workshop polishes and seals the furniture to your specified shade, tone, and sheen.
